# ⚛️ Nuclear Physics Applications in Medicine

<p align="center">
  <em>An educational project covering the physics foundations, imaging techniques,
  and therapeutic applications of nuclear physics in modern medicine.</em>
</p>

## 📖 Overview

Nuclear medicine flips the logic of conventional X-ray imaging: instead of a machine emitting
radiation, **the patient becomes the source of radiation** after injection of a radiotracer.
This presentation walks through the complete physical chain — from radioactive decay and
radiation–matter interactions to clinical imaging systems and cancer therapy.

Presented on **May 11, 2026** for the School of Applied and Engineering Physics,
Université Mohammed VI Polytechnique (UM6P).

## 🗂️ Contents

### 1. Physics Foundations
- Radioactivity and types of nuclear decay (α, β, γ)
- Interaction of radiation with biological matter (photoelectric effect, Compton scattering)
- Radiation dosimetry
- Criteria for choosing medical radioisotopes (half-life, emission type, photon energy,
  biological targeting) — Tc-99m, F-18, I-131

### 2. Nuclear Imaging
- **Planar scintigraphy** — radiotracer design (carrier molecule + radionuclide) and the
  Anger gamma camera: lead collimator, NaI(Tl) scintillation crystal, PMT array, and
  weighted-average Anger positioning logic
- **SPECT** — rotating camera, 60–120 projections, tomographic reconstruction
  (Filtered Back Projection vs. OSEM)
- **PET** — positron annihilation (2 × 511 keV), coincidence detection and lines of response,
  ¹⁸F-FDG and the Warburg effect, Standardized Uptake Value (SUV)

### 3. Nuclear Therapy
- External beam radiotherapy (LINAC, bremsstrahlung, photon vs. electron therapy)
- Brachytherapy
- Targeted radionuclide therapy (TRT)

### 4. Current Challenges
Physics limits and technological challenges of each modality.
